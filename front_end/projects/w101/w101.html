WORK IN PROGRESS: W101 Demo and RL Agent

Demonstrating Web Development and Reinforcement Learning.

Overview

In the video game Wizard101, players can battle enemies in a 2-sided card game. Cards can deal damage, heal, enhance future cards plays, and more. The player's team wins when all enemies have 0 health, and vise versa. Associated with each player and card is a "school" of magic: Life, Death, Fire, Ice, Storm, Myth, and Balance. In this project, I set out to create competitive agents that utilize effective card combinations.
